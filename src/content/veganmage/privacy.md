# Vegan Mage — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 23 July 2026

Vegan Mage is a free browser extension made by an independent developer. There's no account or sign-in, and it doesn't use your name or email. Contact: veganmage@proton.me.

Most of what Vegan Mage does happens locally in your browser — reading and displaying page content stays on your device and isn't sent anywhere. It only works on sites where you've explicitly enabled it (by right-clicking and turning it on). On those sites it reads the content you're currently viewing so it can show it in the side panel, refreshing as you click around the page — so it's keeping up with what you're looking at, not reading just once. It doesn't run on sites you haven't enabled it for, and it doesn't track which sites you visit. The one exception is Cast — see below for how that works.

## What it works with

On a site where you've enabled it, Vegan Mage reads the content you're currently viewing — updating as you click through the page — so it can display it in the side panel and help you work with it. This all happens on your device. It also stores only what it needs to provide the functionality shown in the extension — such as your preferences and the version of the terms you accepted. All of it lives in your browser.

## Cast

Vegan Mage doesn't process what you send through Cast itself, and doesn't send it to its own server. When you use Cast, data — which can include text from the page you're viewing — is handed off to a separate browser extension that you install and connect to Vegan Mage. From there, it's out of Vegan Mage's hands: what that connected extension collects, sends to third parties, uses, or retains is governed entirely by its own Privacy Policy, not this one — review that extension's policy separately for the specifics. Vegan Mage doesn't keep a copy of what's handed off.

## How long things are kept

Your settings remain until you clear the extension's data or uninstall it. Data sent through Cast, and anything returned by it, aren't retained by Vegan Mage; how long the connected extension or its third parties keep anything is covered by that extension's own Privacy Policy.

## Your choices

Clear the extension's data or uninstall it to remove everything it stores. Turn off Cast, or disconnect the connected extension, in Vegan Mage's settings to stop data from being handed off. Email veganmage@proton.me with any question.

## Children

Vegan Mage isn't directed to anyone under 18, and doesn't knowingly collect data from minors.

## Security

Keeping your content on your own device avoids the risks that come with transmitting it. That said, no system is completely secure, so absolute security can't be guaranteed.

## Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest version, and important changes will be surfaced in the extension.
